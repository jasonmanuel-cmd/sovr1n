Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of items do you deliver?
We specialize in oversized, bulky, or fragile items that don't fit in a standard car. This includes furniture, large appliances, bulk restaurant supplies, and event setups.
Do you handle Facebook Marketplace pickups?
Yes. If you buy a couch, dining set, or dresser online, we coordinate the pickup with the seller and deliver it safely to your door.
Can you help local businesses on a recurring basis?
Absolutely. We offer scheduled routes for restaurants, caterers, farmers market vendors, and local retailers to handle their specific logistical needs.
How does pricing work?
Pricing is based on distance, item size, and handling requirements (e.g., stairs). You'll receive a clear, upfront quote before booking.
