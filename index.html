About sovr1n

Our Mission

Sovr1n is a community-focused marketplace platform designed to strengthen local commerce in Kern County. We connect buyers, sellers, drivers, and service providers—keeping resources and revenue local instead of flowing to distant corporate platforms.

Why Sovr1n?

✓
Fair Pricing — Transparent 10% platform fee (not 25-40%)
✓
Driver Income — Drivers keep ~90% of agreed prices
✓
Built-in Contracts — Every deal is documented and protected
✓
Community Trust — Mutual ratings, secure messaging, verified profiles

Who We Serve

Buyers: Need items delivered? Browse local marketplace items with guaranteed delivery contracts.

Shop Owners: Sell produce, goods, and services. Set your own delivery options and pricing.

Drivers: Make money on your schedule. Accept loads that fit your vehicle and earn competitive rates.

Service Providers: Reach local customers without corporate middlemen taking half your earnings.

Independent Resources, Local Confidence

Sovr1n is built for Bakersfield, by people who understand Kern County. We're not a VC-funded app chasing growth at any cost—we're a platform committed to sustainable, community-benefiting commerce.

FAQ & Guidelines

How do contracts work?

When a buyer and driver agree on a load, sovr1n generates a downloadable contract with both names, pricing, delivery timeframe, and item description. Both parties can review and accept. Contracts protect all parties legally.

What are the vehicle classes?

Cars/Pickups: Sedans, SUVs, pickup trucks for small loads (fees: $2/mo or $20/yr subscription)

Hot Shot: F250/350 with flatbed trailer, max ~15,000-20,000 lbs (fees: 2%)

Class A: Full-size semi trucks with trailers (fees: 2%)

Weight limits by vehicle?

Each vehicle class has legal weight limits. Drivers are responsible for knowing their vehicle's capacity and local DOT regulations. Exceeding limits is the driver's legal liability, not sovr1n's.

Insurance requirement?

All drivers must provide proof of insurance covering loads. Your insurance must be valid and current. Sovr1n validates proof at signup but does not guarantee coverage.

Pictures required?

Before pickup: Driver takes photos of load to document condition (protects both parties from damage claims).

At delivery: Buyer signs off confirming no damage received. This completes the contract.

What area does sovr1n serve?

Sovr1n focuses on Kern County, California—primarily Bakersfield, Shafter, and Lamont areas. Shop owners and drivers set their own delivery radius (e.g., "within 30 miles of Bakersfield").

How do ratings work?

After each completed transaction, both parties can rate each other 1-5 stars with optional comments. Ratings build trust and reputation over time. All users see average ratings.

What if something goes wrong?

Refer to your signed contract—it's legally binding. Disputes should be resolved between parties directly. Sovr1n can facilitate communication but is not responsible for resolving conflicts.

Driver Responsibility: You are responsible for knowing all local, state, and federal transportation laws. Any violations of regulations are your legal responsibility, not sovr1n's.
